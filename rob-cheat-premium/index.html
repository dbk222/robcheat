Good to know

Before you choose.

Quick answers about access, activation and managing your purchase.

When does my access period begin?

Check the activation and expiry details shown for your license in your account. If your license does not show the expected period, contact support before purchasing another key.

Where can I find my license?

Sign in to your ROB CHEAT account and open the license area to view the key and its status.

What happens if checkout succeeds but my license is missing?

Keep your Stripe receipt and contact support with the email used at checkout. Do not share your password or full payment details.

How do I get help?

Use the Discord link in the navigation or footer and include your order reference when asking about a purchase.
