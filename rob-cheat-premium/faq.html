Frequently asked
questions.

Quick answers about the website, account experience and getting in touch with support.

What is ROB CHEAT?

ROB CHEAT is the name of this website and its community-facing brand. Please review the product description and applicable policies before making any purchase.

Where can I see the available plans?

Visit the Plans section on the homepage to view the current options and displayed prices.

Is checkout live?

At the moment, the checkout on this preview site is a demonstration only. No payment is processed and no order is created. A live payment provider must be configured before launch.

How do I contact support?

Use the Contact / Support page. The form is currently a frontend preview and is not connected to a support inbox.

How are refunds handled?

Refund eligibility and the process must be defined in the published Terms of Service and applicable consumer law. Please contact support before purchase if you need clarification.

How do I protect my account?

Use a unique, strong password, never share credentials, and contact support if you notice activity you do not recognise.

Where can I read the legal policies?

See Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. These pages are draft templates and must be completed before the website goes live.
