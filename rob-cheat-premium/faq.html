Frequently asked
questions.

Clear information about ROB RUN access plans, account setup, checkout, license delivery and support.

What is ROB RUN?

ROB RUN is the product presented by ROB CHEAT. The website provides account access, plan selection and a license-management flow for customers.

Which features are included in ROB RUN?

ROB RUN's listed toolkit includes Players ESP, Vehicles ESP, Custom Range, Player Filtering, Loot ESP, Loot Filtering, Aim Assist, Radar and Custom Colors. Availability and behavior may vary by version; check the current product information before purchase.

Can I customize ESP range, filters and colors?

The feature set includes Custom Range, Player Filtering, Loot Filtering and Custom Colors, allowing supported display settings to be tailored to your preferences.

What does Radar and Aim Assist do?

Radar provides a compact spatial overview, while Aim Assist offers configurable supported targeting behavior. Review the in-product settings and current documentation for details and limitations.

Which ROB RUN access plans are available?

The current plans shown on the website are Daily (€2 for 1 day), Weekly (€5 for 7 days), Monthly (€10 for 30 days) and Lifetime (€20 one time). Check the plans section for current availability.

How do I purchase access?

Create an account or sign in first, choose an access plan and follow the checkout flow. Checkout is handled through the connected payment provider; the final amount and terms are shown before payment confirmation.

Where can I manage my account and license?

Use the Account page to register or sign in. After authentication, use the account dashboard to review your account and license information.

How do I get help with a payment or license?

Contact the ROB CHEAT team through the support page or join the Discord community. Include your account email and a clear description of the issue—never send your password.

How do I keep my account secure?

Use a unique password, do not share your credentials or license key publicly, and contact support if you notice activity you do not recognise.

Where are the legal policies?

Read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before purchasing. They explain the applicable terms and how account information is handled.
